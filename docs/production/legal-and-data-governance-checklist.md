# Legal and data governance checklist

This is an implementation checklist, not legal advice or final contract language.
C6 remains open until qualified counsel and accountable business owners approve
the documents for every supported jurisdiction and the customer accepts them.

## Required approvals and documents

- [ ] Terms of Service and authorized acceptance record.
- [ ] Privacy Notice identifying controller/processor roles and lawful bases.
- [ ] Data Processing Agreement, security schedule, and cross-border transfer terms.
- [ ] Retention/deletion schedule for accounting, identity, audit, support, backup,
      AI conversation, and telemetry data, including legal holds.
- [ ] Customer data export, account termination, deletion, and verification process.
- [ ] Breach response, notification decision owner, and jurisdiction deadlines.
- [ ] Hosting, monitoring, email, support, and AI subprocessor list with locations.
- [ ] OpenAI/Gemini disclosure and approval before changing provider from `rules`.
- [ ] AI disclaimer: suggestions require authorized human review and never post automatically.
- [ ] Accounting reliance disclaimer and customer responsibility for professional review.
- [ ] Supported currencies, jurisdictions, chart flexibility, ledger/report workflows,
      exports, availability assumptions, and record-retention responsibilities.
- [ ] Explicit exclusions unless separately implemented and certified: statutory
      tax/VAT filing, payroll, bank reconciliation, source-document attachments,
      customer invoicing/accounts receivable workflow, and regulatory submissions.
- [ ] Support hours, severity definitions, response targets, SLA/SLO, maintenance,
      escalation, accessibility, and supported-browser policy.
- [ ] Subscription invoicing, renewal, trial, suspension, cancellation, refund,
      non-payment, data access during suspension, and data-exit policy.
- [ ] Data subject request intake, identity verification, fulfillment, and audit trail.

## Objective GO evidence

Retain document versions, counsel approval, business/accounting scope approval,
subprocessor/privacy review, customer acceptance timestamps, support ownership,
and billing/data-exit procedures. Placeholders or this checklist do not close C6.
